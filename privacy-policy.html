Privacy Policy: NoBS - Intermittent Fasting

Effective: 31 October 2026 · Developer: Pixel Ocean Dev Studios

1. What the app stores

Your fasting schedule, start and end times, and fasting history. All of it is saved only on your phone.

2. No account

There's no sign-up or sign-in.

3. No data collection

We don't collect, send or share any personal data. There's no analytics, no tracking and no ads.

4. Deleting data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

5. Not medical advice

NoBS is a habit timer. Check with a health professional before you start fasting, especially if you're pregnant or have a medical condition.

6. Audience

The app is meant for adults (18+).

7. Future ads

If ads are added, this policy and the app's Google Play Data safety section will be updated first.
