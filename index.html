Privacy Policy: NoBS - Intermittent Fasting

Effective: 22 November 2026 · Developer: Pixel Ocean Dev Studios

1. What the app stores

Your fasting schedule, start and end times, and fasting history. All of it is saved only on your phone.

2. No account

There's no sign-up or sign-in.

3. Feedback you choose to send

The app doesn't collect anything in the background. There's no analytics, no tracking and no ads.

The only time data leaves your phone is when you open Send feedback and tap Submit. What's sent then:

  • the title and description you type
  • your email address, only if you choose to add it, and only so we can reply
  • the app version and build, your Android version and your phone model, so we can look into problems

Your fasting schedule, times and history are never sent.

Feedback is sent over an encrypted connection to a Google Form owned by Pixel Ocean Dev Studios. Google stores it for us under the Google Privacy Policy. We don't sell it or share it with anyone else. We keep feedback for up to 12 months, then delete it. To have your feedback deleted sooner, email pixeloceandevstudios@gmail.com.

4. Deleting data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

5. Not medical advice

NoBS is a habit timer. Check with a health professional before you start fasting, especially if you're pregnant or have a medical condition.

6. Audience

The app is meant for adults (18+).

7. Future ads

If ads are added, this policy and the app's Google Play Data safety section will be updated first.
